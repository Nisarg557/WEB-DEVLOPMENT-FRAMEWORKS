OFFLINE PAYMENT DETAILS

To make an offline payment, please follow the instructions below:

  1. Visit Our College Fee Payment Center

  2. Bring your student ID

  3. Fill out the form available at the desk near the payment counter containing necessary details.

  4. Submit the form along with the payment to the cashier.

  5. Keep the receipt provided by the cashier for your records.
