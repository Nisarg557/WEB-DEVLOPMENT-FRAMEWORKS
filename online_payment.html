ONLINE PAYMENT DETAILS

To make an online payment, please follow the instructions below:

  1. Visit Our College Fee Payment Online Portal

  2. Log in to your account.

  3. Fill out the form available at the portal containing necessary details.

  4. Submit the form along with the payment though the avilable UPI options.

  5. Keep the receipt provided by the web for any further use.
